      How to play

      Each day there are two mystery U.S. senators to find:

      • Normal: a well-known senator.
      • Hard: a lesser-known senator.

      You start with one clue. Each clue is more specific than the last. After every clue you can:

      • Guess: search the database by name, and filter by state or party. A wrong guess reveals the next clue.
      • Skip: reveal the next clue without guessing.

      The fewer clues you need, the better. A wrong guess after the final clue ends the round.

      If a clue in today's puzzle gives away the senator's state or party, that filter stays hidden until you reach the clue.

      A new puzzle comes out every day at midnight, your local time.
