      How to play

      Each day there are two mystery U.S. senators to find:

      • Normal: a well-known senator.
      • Hard: a lesser-known senator.

      Each seat in the chamber hides a clue, and each clue is more specific than the last. After every clue you can:

      • Guess: search the database by name. Results show each senator's party and state, e.g. (D-GA). A wrong guess reveals the next clue.
      • Skip: press and hold to reveal the next clue without guessing.

      The fewer clues you need, the better. A wrong guess after the final clue ends the round. Press and hold Give up to see the answer.

      Once a clue gives away the senator's party or state, search results stop showing it.

      A new puzzle comes out every day at midnight, your local time.
